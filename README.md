# ADR Identification Basics

## Task 1 – Adverse Drug Reaction Identification

### 1. Patient Information

- Patient: Female
- Age: 45 years
- Symptoms: Rash, itching, nausea and dizziness

### 2. Medication History

- Amoxicillin – started 5 days ago
- Paracetamol – as needed

### 3. Timeline

- Day -0: Amoxicillin initiated
- Day -2: Symptoms appeared
- Day 5: Symptoms persisted/worsened

### 4. Suspected ADR

The patient's rash and itching are suggestive of a possible
hypersensitivity/allergic reaction associated with amoxicillin.

### 5. Suspected Drug

Amoxicillin

### 6. Causality Assessment

The temporal relationship between initiation of amoxicillin
and development of symptoms supports a possible drug-related
adverse reaction.

Additional clinical information would be required for a
definitive causality assessment.

### 7. Conclusion

Amoxicillin is suspected to be responsible for a possible
hypersensitivity-related ADR characterized primarily by
rash and itching, with associated nausea and dizziness.
